# The provider's RBAC is generated from markers and holds leader election in the manager's namespace, on its own Lease

Decided 2026-11-11.

## Decision

- The ClusterRole is generated from the `+kubebuilder:rbac` markers into [`config/rbac/role.yaml`](../../config/rbac/role.yaml), except leader election.
- [`leader_election_role.yaml`](../../config/rbac/leader_election_role.yaml) grants Leases and Events in the manager's namespace only, and `get` and `update` of Leases only on the one named by `LeaderElectionID`.
  So its Role and ClusterRole grant it no Lease in `kube-node-lease`, and in its own namespace `get` and `update` on its own Lease only, and `delete` on none.
- `create` cannot be limited by name ([RBAC][k8s-rbac-names]), so the provider can still create a Lease under any free name.

## Why

- Lodia grants leader election in a Role in its own namespace, with `get` and `update` only on its own Lease ([Lodia's Role][lo-leader-election]).
- With Leases and Events in the ClusterRole, the provider's ServiceAccount could rewrite every Lease of the management cluster, the nodes' heartbeats in `kube-node-lease` and other controllers' leader Leases included, while the only Event it records is leader election's, in its own namespace.

## Rejected

- Markers for Leases and Events, which put them in the generated ClusterRole, bound cluster-wide — the reach of the second point of Why.

## Consequences

- The ClusterRole's `create` and `get` on Secrets in every namespace, which the copies in each Cluster's namespace need, still let the provider create a `kubernetes.io/service-account-token` Secret for any ServiceAccount and read the token filled in for it ([validation][k8s-sa-secret], [tokens controller][k8s-sa-tokens], [authentication][k8s-sa-auth]), so the limits above contain the provider's mistakes, not a compromised provider.

## Code

- `config/rbac/role.yaml` is generated by `make generate`; `config/rbac/leader_election_role.yaml` and `leader_election_role_binding.yaml` are written by hand.
- `internal/controller/suite_test.go` runs the controller as a user bound to the ClusterRole (`boundToTheClusterRole`).
- `internal/controller/rbac_test.go` binds that user, in the namespace where a leader-elected manager elects, to the Role (`boundToTheLeaderElectionRole`), and holds: `TestTheRoleCoversLeaderElectionAndTheMetricsFilter`, `TestTheLeaderElectionRoleHoldsNothingBeyondItsLeaseAndEventRules`, `TestTheLeaderElectionRoleIsBoundOnlyToTheManager`, `TestTheClusterRoleGrantsNothingOnLeasesOrEvents`, `TestLeasesAndEventsAreGrantedOnlyInTheLeaderElectionNamespace` and `TestALeaseUnderAFreeNameCanBeCreatedButAnotherControllersCannotBeReadChangedOrDeleted`.
- `test/components/main_test.go` — `TestTheManagerReachesOnlyItsLeaderElectionRoleAndItsClusterRole`.
